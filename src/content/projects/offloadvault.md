## The problem

Photo libraries lock people into one provider's storage. Anyone keeping their own buckets had no gallery experience close to the big clouds.

## What we are building

A gallery built on your own object storage. Bring your own bucket across 15 providers, with search, sharing, and organisation, and no lock-in to a single host.
