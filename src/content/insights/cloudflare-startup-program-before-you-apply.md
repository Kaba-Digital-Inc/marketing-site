*This is general information, written in October 2026 from public Cloudflare pages. We are not affiliated with the program, Cloudflare decides who is approved, and the terms change. Always check the current details on [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/) before you plan around any number.*

Cloudflare offers credits to qualifying startups to offset usage-based services such as Workers, storage, and AI. If you are building on the platform, it can be worth real money in your first year. Here is how to approach it.

## The tiers: check which version you are looking at

The public Cloudflare pages currently show two different credit ladders.

| Page | Tiers shown |
| --- | --- |
| [Main startups page](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/) | $10,000 / $100,000 / $350,000, by amount raised and affiliated partners |
| [Regional page](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-ca/forstartups/) and [program announcement](https://blog.cloudflare.com/expanding-cloudflares-startup-program/) | $5,000 / $25,000 / $100,000 / $250,000 |

We cannot tell you which is current for your region, so use the application form as the source of truth and do not quote an amount to your investors until you have it in writing.

## What the program generally asks for

Across the Cloudflare pages, applicants are generally expected to:

- be building a software product or service, within the program's age limit for the tier,
- have a working public website and a business email that matches the domain,
- show an active presence on LinkedIn, X, or GitHub,
- be a first-time applicant, and
- fit the funding criteria for the tier, which at the upper tiers refer to approved venture capital and accelerator partners.

The application is made by the startup itself: create a free account, complete the form, and wait for the confirmation email. The Cloudflare page says review typically takes up to 48 hours.

## The route for AI companies

The regional page lists a top tier for high-growth companies, and it names AI companies specifically. The criterion it gives is that you are building a mission critical AI application, or that you take part in the Workers Launchpad program. It is not a separate AI tier, and the wording can change, but it means an AI product built on the platform does not necessarily need a particular investor to reach the higher credit levels.

## What the credits cover

Credits apply to usage-based services, and the program text lists exclusions and caps for some products, such as storage limits and certain services that may be temporarily unavailable. Read the exclusions before you design around a service. Credits are time-limited, so plan what you will run during that period and what it will cost after the credits end.

## How to prepare a strong application

- **Be specific about what you are building.** A clear one-line description beats a vague mission statement.
- **Have a live site and real presence.** Reviewers can only judge what they can see.
- **Show the workload.** If you are building agents or AI features on Cloudflare, say which products you will use.
- **Know your funding position.** The tier depends on it, so be accurate.

## Plan the architecture before the credits arrive

Credits are easy to waste on a design that is expensive to run. Before you spend them, decide how your agents hold state, how long tasks run, and what each task costs.
